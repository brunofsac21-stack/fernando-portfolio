  threejs, react, nextjs, typescript, nodejs, python, google): repositório
  [GLINCKER/thesvg](https://github.com/GLINCKER/thesvg) (MIT).
- power-apps, power-automate, power-platform: ícones oficiais da Microsoft, via
  [aidevme/microsoft-cloud-product-icons](https://github.com/aidevme/microsoft-cloud-product-icons) (CC BY 4.0).
- Ícones genéricos `lucide-*`: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC), com traço recolorido para #111.

As marcas pertencem aos respectivos donos e são usadas só para identificar as ferramentas.
